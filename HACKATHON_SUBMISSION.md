# SANGYAN submission notes (Track C: Investor Education for Bharat)

## One-line pitch
A safe, practice-only trading-terminal and scam-spotting game that teaches market resilience in six Indian languages.

## Problem
First-time investors meet jargon, panic mornings and sophisticated scams (fake advisers, deepfake finfluencers, OTP phishing) with no safe place to practise.

## Solution
- Terminal-style simulator with plain-language glossary on every term.
- "Subah ke 9 Baje" scores emotional resilience, not returns.
- Adaptive scam spotter built on realistic WhatsApp, SMS and call patterns.
- Voice explainer on every section, in the chosen language.
- Budget-phone friendly: single file, offline-capable, no sign-up.

## Demo script (3 minutes)
