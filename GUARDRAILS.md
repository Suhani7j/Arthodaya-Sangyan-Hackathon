# Guardrails

1. **No advice.** No buy, sell or hold suggestions and no price predictions. The Guide refuses these before any model is called.
2. **No broker integration** and no real-money flows. The wallet is virtual and cannot be withdrawn.
3. **No profit leaderboards.** XP, streaks and badges reward learning and reflection only.
4. **Honest framing.** Every outcome screen states that real markets are unpredictable. "Resilient" is never presented as "will make money".
5. **Privacy.** No login, OTP or Demat linking. No personal data stored server-side. Typed questions to the Guide are never sent for translation.
6. **Scam content** shows red flags and safe actions only; it must not read as a template for running a scam.
`npm test` checks 4 of these automatically where possible.
